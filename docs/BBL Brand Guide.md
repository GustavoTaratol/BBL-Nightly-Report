# BBL Bombastic Basketball League — Brand Guide

## Reference

The primary reference is `assets/bbl-bombastic-logo.jpg`. It is the source of truth for the nightly report cards and future league graphics.

## Visual identity

- **Mood:** energetic, competitive, loud, and playful—like a basketball broadcast graphic with league-chat personality.
- **Canvas:** deep navy background with generous spacing and strong card separation.
- **Logo:** use the full BBL Bombastic Basketball League mark; do not redraw, crop, or replace it with a generic basketball icon.
- **Typography:** bold condensed sports-display feel for headlines; clean sans-serif for data and captions.
- **Imagery:** real player headshots are acceptable; avoid AI-generated portraits, decorative stock photography, and unnecessary graphic clutter.

## Nightly report rules

- The two primary graphics are **Top 10 Performances** and **Top 10 Worst Performances**.
- Include the BBL logo, report date, player headshots, manager names, BBL points, and honorable or dishonorable mentions.
- The performance card uses flame orange; the worst-performance card uses violet with cyan supporting accents.
- Keep the report readable in a group chat: large player names, large scores, short summaries, and no dense paragraphs.
